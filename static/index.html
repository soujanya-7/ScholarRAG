SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The ScholarRAG Engineering Blueprint

Key mathematical and architectural principles to master for AI engineering interviews.

1. Layout-Aware PDF Parsing & Recursive Chunking

Standard character splitting breaks mathematical formulas and multi-column tables. ScholarRAG extracts page-by-page metadata, detects section boundaries, and maintains a 120-character linguistic overlap to preserve contextual continuity across chunk splits.

2. Bi-Encoders vs. Cross-Encoders

Bi-Encoders encode query $Q$ and doc $D$ into independent vectors for $O(1)$ search lookup. Cross-Encoders pass $(Q, D)$ jointly through full multi-head self-attention layers to compute deep token-to-token interactions.

3. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)

Dense vector search produces cosine similarities $\in [0, 1]$, while BM25 produces unbounded scores $[0, \infty)$. RRF combines their rank distributions without fragile score normalization:

$$RRF(d) = \sum_{m \in M} \frac{1}{k + \text{rank}_m(d)}$$

4. Hypothetical Document Embeddings (HyDE)

User questions are short and interrogative; research papers are formal and explanatory. HyDE asks the LLM to hypothesize an academic excerpt first, embedding that answer to land directly inside the document's vector cluster.
